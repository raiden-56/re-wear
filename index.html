Questions, answered.

Is pickup really free?

Yes. Doorstep pickup is always free. We never charge a pickup, convenience or collection fee, and we never deduct pickup costs from your earnings.

Is there a minimum quantity?

Not by default. Pickup availability depends on whether we serve your pincode and on slot capacity. If an area ever needs a minimum, it is published on that area's booking page before you book — never applied as a fee or deduction.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, any time before the collector starts the trip — from your bookings page, at no cost.

How is the weight calculated?

Gross weight (clothes in bags) minus tare weight (empty bags/containers) gives the net weight. The collector enters weight per category and photographs the digital scale. You see every number before you approve.

What if some clothes aren't accepted?

Items we can't accept (wet, soiled, innerwear, etc.) are weighed separately as rejected weight and are not paid. You can keep them or let us dispose of them responsibly.

What if I disagree with the valuation?

Don't approve it. You can reject it and raise a dispute — our team reviews the scale photos with you. Nothing is paid or finalised without your approval.

How do I get paid?

Directly to your UPI ID (e.g. name@okaxis). There's no wallet balance to withdraw. You'll see the status — approved, processing, successful — and the bank reference (UTR) once the transfer completes.
