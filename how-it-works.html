How it works

From your wardrobe to a second life — transparently.

Every step is recorded: who collected, what was weighed, which rate applied, when you approved and when the money reached your bank.

  1. Step 1

    Book in two minutes

    Choose Quick Sell by KG, Premium Resale, Recycling or Donation. Pick categories, estimate weight, see live rates and an instant estimate. Choose a free 2-hour pickup slot.

  2. Step 2

    Collector verifies at your door

    You get a 4-digit booking code in the app. The collector must enter it before starting — so you always know it's a genuine ReWear pickup.

  3. Step 3

    Transparent digital weighing

    Bags are weighed on a calibrated scale (gross), empty bags are weighed (tare), and the difference is your net weight. Each category is weighed separately.

  4. Step 4

    Evidence for every number

    The collector photographs the scale display. Photos, weights and rates are attached to your booking for you to review.

  5. Step 5

    You approve the valuation

    Approve in the app, or read out the one-time code we text you. Disagree? Reject it — nothing is finalised without you.

  6. Step 6

    Direct UPI payment

    Payment goes straight to your UPI ID. Track it from approved to successful, with the bank's UTR and a downloadable receipt.

  7. Step 7

    Sorted at our hub

    Every batch is received, re-weighed and graded: premium, standard reusable, repairable, recyclable or upcycling.

  8. Step 8

    A second life

    Premium pieces are resold individually; wearables go to thrift partners and NGOs; the rest goes to verified textile recyclers.

Ready when you are.

Pickup is free. Approving is up to you.
