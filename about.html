About ReWear

Clothes are too valuable to throw away.

India discards millions of tonnes of textiles every year. Most of it is still wearable — or recyclable into new fibre. ReWear makes clearing your wardrobe simple and fairly paid, and makes sure every garment finds its best next use.

What we believe

Fair and transparent. You see the rate, the scale photo, the tare and the net weight. You approve before anything is final.

Free, without fine print. Pickup is free, permanently. We never recover logistics costs from your earnings.

Paid like a professional. Directly to your UPI ID, with a bank reference — not points or a wallet you have to withdraw from.

Circular by design. Resale first, then reuse, upcycling and recycling with verified partners. Donations are kept separate from commercial sales.
